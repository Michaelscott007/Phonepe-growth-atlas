EXPERIMENT DESIGN

Merchant Activation Sprint

Do not assume low merchant density is the problem. Test whether a tighter acquisition-to-first-transaction journey increases activated merchants in high-demand districts.

01

Target

New or recently onboarded merchants in the selected districts, stratified by category and baseline activity.

02

Variant

Faster activation sequence: guided QR setup, first-transaction nudge, education, and timed follow-up.

03

Primary metric

Share of onboarded merchants completing a first qualified transaction within 7 days.

04

Secondary

30-day active merchant rate, transactions per activated merchant, time-to-first-transaction.

05

Guardrails

Fraud/risk flags, support contacts per merchant, incentive cost, merchant opt-out/churn.

06

Rollout

5% holdout → 20% → 50%; expand only if activation lift persists without guardrail deterioration.

METHOD

What the model actually does

Demand index

District transactions per registered user ÷ Maharashtra district mean.

Acceptance index

District registered merchants per 1,000 registered users ÷ Maharashtra district mean.

Gap

Demand index minus acceptance index. Positive values flag demand that is high relative to registered merchant density.

Momentum

Q2 transaction count compared with Q1 2026 using the same restated Pulse release.

Important: registered users and registered merchants are cumulative registered counts, not active-user or active-merchant measures. This analysis is a hypothesis generator, not causal proof or a production recommendation.
